# Boston waterfront treatment

Research and source checks: 15 November 2026. Supplied Street View references cover Piers Park I/II, East Pier Drive, LoPresti Park, Charlestown Navy Yard, Coast Guard Base Boston, Burroughs Wharf, the downtown Harborwalk, Pier 4/Commonwealth Pier, Boston Fish Pier and Conley Terminal.

## Geometry and accuracy

`scripts/build-waterfront.py` builds `src/data/waterfront.json` from the shipped Boston hydro and open-space survey polygons. Hand-selected site extents identify a wharf; they do **not** replace its coastline. Surface polygons exclude the mapped water, and retaining-wall lines come from that same hydro boundary. This preserves the narrow slips between adjacent piers. Overlapping selectors give the earlier, more specific site ownership.

The projection is the existing Massachusetts State Plane EPSG:2249 conversion: world x is east and z is south. Dimensions, building placement and shore outlines from the existing dataset are retained. The following are visual interpretations, not engineering measurements: landward site cuts, pavement patterns, lawn subdivisions, bench/lamp spacing, quay construction details, wall heights, container inventory and crane parking positions. Park lawns sit inside paved perimeter paths; cached OpenStreetMap footways are removed from the lawn masks. Paving and lawns are disjoint polygons to prevent depth fighting.

Some source DEM samples remain several metres above the harbor plane even 10–30 m inside mapped water. `waterCuts` therefore records only mapped water within 100 m of hard quay edges, excluding ground-road ribbons with the same total 6.1 m allowance used by `water-pavement.ts`. Rendering can remove the fictitious grassy offshore slope without changing the surveyed dry-land footprints or road approaches. This is an interpretation of inconsistent source terrain and hydro, not a new bathymetric survey.

## Fish Pier and dry docks

Massport's restoration brief gives Fish Pier's approximate envelope as 300 by 1,200 feet and describes two matching main buildings plus the third Exchange building. The custom hall geometry follows existing building/source positions. The supplied 2026 Street View image determines the prominent entrance-face color composition and lettering; its people and exact vehicle arrangement are not copied.

Dry Dock 1 uses cached OSM way 226524669. NPS describes granite construction, a later concrete seaward extension and a steel caisson replacing the wooden one; its final stated length is 404 feet. The rendered wet, stepped basin preserves the current simulation water level. Dry Dock 2 is extracted from the merged Boston hydro polygon, with a perpendicular mouth cut estimated from its mapped sidewalls. The NPS landscape report records its caisson being moved to Portsmouth in 1976, so it remains an open flooded basin. The masonry courses are illustrative, not measured restoration drawings.

Pier 1's outer tip is included in the naval hardstand all the way to its mapped quay. The [NPS master development environmental assessment](https://parkplanning.nps.gov/showFile.cfm?projectID=90626&sfid=627268) identifies parking and pedestrian walkways around Building 109. The tip's former grass appearance came from an incomplete surface selector, rather than an actual lawn in the open-space survey. Small mapped Navy Yard Grounds lawns remain intact inland.

## Conley Terminal

[Massport's 2026 fact sheet](https://www.massport.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/POB_CONLEY_Fact-Sheet-2026.pdf) establishes seven active container cranes: three with 202-foot outreach and four with 150-foot outreach, within a 130-acre terminal. [Massport's equipment procurement supplement](https://www.massport.com/sites/default/files/2023-12/m724-supplemental-package-10-30-23.pdf) distinguishes the pale cranes 1/2, dark-blue cranes 3/4 and newer pale cranes 5–7, and explains the low-profile design required by Logan's flight paths. The important silhouette is the horizontal sliding boom, not a tall conventional hinged boom.

The modern Berth 10 extension was absent from the older source shoreline. Its new raised, pile-supported wharf is modeled west of the original straight quay. [Massport's engineering award submission](https://aapa.cms-plus.com/files/New_Berth_10_Massport_2021_Facilities_Engineering_Award_Submission_FINAL%20%282%29.pdf) and [CMAA's project account](https://www.cmaanet.org/conley-terminal-modernization-program-new-berth-10-construction) describe a roughly 1,275–1,300-foot wharf about 108–110 feet wide. The renderer's rear overlap closes the old hydro indentation; the old interior coastline is removed from the generic seawall list.

Seven ship-to-shore cranes, regular east–west container rows, cross-aisles and yard gantries create the working terminal. Individual stacks, colors, tier counts and gantry positions are illustrative rather than a current cargo manifest. [Massport's terminal environmental filing](https://www.massport.com/sites/default/files/2023-10/conleyenf_compiled.pdf) provides the container-yard arrangement context.

## Reproduction and limitations

Run `.venv-data/bin/python scripts/build-waterfront.py`. The generator requires the existing cached `osm-metro-paths.json` and `osm-metro-water.json`; no new network request is made during generation. Its checked-in output is sufficient for runtime and tests.

Run `.venv-data/bin/python scripts/validate-waterfront.py` to check polygon validity, nonoverlapping site ownership, complete disjoint paving/lawn coverage, source-aligned quays with a valid landward side, dry-dock mouth alignment, and water-only terrain cuts. The current output contains 65 surface parts, 97 quay polylines, two dry docks and 16 water-cut polygons. Spatial comparisons allow only millimetre quantization at the survey boundary.

This is a geographic/material improvement at game scale. It does not claim a cadastral boundary survey, exact present-day landscaping at every parcel, current access restrictions, navigation-grade tides, or a live port equipment inventory. Existing building masses remain except where the dedicated Fish Pier model supplies the researched architecture. No remote photographs are shipped as game textures.

For visual checks, build the app and start a preview, then run `PORT=4180 DRIVE=1 node scripts/capture-waterfront.mjs` using the preview's port. The six-stop tour writes screenshots and browser diagnostics under `artifacts/waterfront`, and drives through Fish Pier's central aisle. `SITE=navy-yard,chelsea` selects specific stops; `MOBILE=1` exercises the mobile renderer in browser emulation. Physical-device performance requires a separate hardware check.
