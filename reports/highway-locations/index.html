1. Western Avenue / Soldiers Field Road

Reported: Bridge deck blocked the lower road.

Cause and fix: The clearance solver treated a shared XY source vertex as a same-level junction, even though the two roads have different source levels. The underpass generator now lowers the connected lower roadway with a continuous grade and retains the bridge and its approaches.

History: October 31: shared-vertex exception in water-crossing levelling; predates the I-90/Storrow addition. 6084df3

2. Charlestown / I-93 Exit 20

Reported: An elevated exit ended at the edge of the survey mesh.

Cause and fix: The source tail of the active exit was missing from the I-93 route inventory. The generator now follows the retained source ways from the measured survey mouth to Cambridge Street; the end-cap rule checks for an actual continuation before closing a survey end.

History: November 7: the original I-93 addition omitted this active tail. November 9 commit 1a608e4 subsequently capped the unfinished survey end. 79ef6ec

3. Atlantic Avenue / Central Artery

Reported: Repeated isolated holes exposed an underground bore.

Cause and fix: Terrain dips were interpreted as new portals. The profile now keeps each buried section continuously covered while preserving its existing portal approaches, then propagates corrected elevations to connected branches.

History: October 30: terrain-based tunnel coverage and portal enclosures; predates the expansion. af36d86

4. South Station Connector

Reported: Two elevated road ends had no continuations.

Cause and fix: The street importer omitted unnamed GIS links while retaining the named bridges they connected. Those source links now belong to the highway route inventory. Their heights match the entire shared pavement at the joins, and the return loop retains the elevated level specified by its source. The solver also rechecks dependent ramps after parent profiles change, preventing steps at their joins.

History: October 28: unnamed GIS roads were skipped by the street importer. The later highway inventory did not recover these two missing links. 479d3c5

5. I-90 / Exit 134A

Reported: A ramp crossed a concrete enclosure around a false opening in the tunnel roof.

Cause and fix: The highway cover classifier promoted short, thinly covered sections to portals even inside an underground mainline. Enclosed spans now retain a roof; generator validation rejects isolated openings inside a bore.

History: November 11: the short-thin-span portal rule was present in the initial I-90/Storrow implementation. 2aa9ec9

6. Cross / Sudbury Street portals

Reported: Buildings retained floating roofs and detached facade fragments.

Cause and fix: The clearance clipper removed walls independently from roofs, and triangle-component pruning discarded valid wall fragments at T-junctions. The shared render/collision geometry now retains those fragments and closes exposed roof cut edges with reveal walls, while preserving the tunnel clearance volume.

History: October 31: shell clearance and fragment pruning; predates the expansion. 47b10b4

7. Leverett Circle / Storrow Drive

Reported: Disconnected elevated pieces overlapped and changed width abruptly.

Cause and fix: The imported survey contained duplicate lower connector components, and old approaches lifted underground source ramps to those components. The generator now retires the bounded duplicate components, uses connected source road alignments, and tapers the widths at shared joins. A height-aware curb check also removes ground curbs that were supported only by an elevated road's footprint.

History: November 3: the Museum repair raised source approaches to disconnected survey geometry; predates the I-90/Storrow addition. 3544e76

8. River Street / Soldiers Field Road

Reported: The river bridge stood at almost the same level as the roadway beneath it.

Cause and fix: The same shared-vertex classification error affected this crossing. The lower roadway now forms a continuous underpass with clearance beneath the existing bridge slab.

History: October 31: the same shared-vertex exception as Western Avenue. 6084df3
